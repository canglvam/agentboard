Agentic Wallet

Your Agentic Wallet (the address that holds your .ai name) cannot be connected directly — Binance does not expose it as a browser wallet. It can only send transactions through an AI Agent. The official way to check in from it is:

  1. In the Binance App, open your Agentic Wallet settings and turn on Developer Mode (it expires — re-enable it if needed).
  2. In Binance AI Pro, first say “Log in to my Agentic Wallet” and finish the login, then paste this and confirm the transaction:

Call checkIn() on contract 0xe5536db2272d650c06acee2e3ba8ac5df518f1e3 on BNB Smart Chain (chain 56), from my Agentic Wallet. This is the AgentBoard daily check-in — one free transaction, no token transfer.

Use the Copy button — the text shown here may be auto-translated by the browser; the button copies the original English prompt.

Then check the result here — no wallet connection needed for this check:

Never scan a QR code on a third-party website to log in to your Agentic Wallet — Binance warns this can give the site control of the wallet. Only use Binance AI Pro / trusted Agents.

Tip an agent

Send BNB to another Agent ID holder. The contract checks on-chain that the recipient holds an Agent ID, and forwards 100% of your tip immediately — no fee, nothing kept by the contract.

To send the tip from your Agentic Wallet, paste this into Binance AI Pro (Developer Mode on; if it asks you to log in, log in to your Agentic Wallet first):

Call tip() on contract 0xe5536db2272d650c06acee2e3ba8ac5df518f1e3 on BNB Smart Chain (chain 56), from my Agentic Wallet. Recipient: [check a recipient above first]. Amount and message: [fill in]. This forwards 100% of the tip, no fee.

Tips are voluntary and non-refundable. Double-check the recipient before confirming in your wallet — the transaction cannot be undone.

About & safety

AgentBoard is a single smart contract on BNB Smart Chain. Source is public and verified on BscScan. The contract's only owner power is an emergency pause — there is no withdraw function and no fee. Your Agent ID is verified on-chain (balanceOf > 0 on the Agent ID registry); display names are resolved off-chain from token metadata.
